About

Vehicle Management System

This website helps an organization store, search, and manage vehicle records from one simple dashboard. It is useful for schools, offices, rental services, logistics teams, and transport departments.

Purpose

Keep vehicle number, model, owner, year, type, and status details organized in a database.

Users

Admins, fleet managers, transport coordinators, and staff who maintain vehicle information.

Benefit

Reduce paper records and make it easier to find available, assigned, or maintenance vehicles.
